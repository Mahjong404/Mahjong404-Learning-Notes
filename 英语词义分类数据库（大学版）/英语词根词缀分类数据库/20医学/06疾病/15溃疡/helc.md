<font color="sky blue">**helcology**</font> n. 溃疡学（即对溃疡的研究）

<font color="sky blue">**helcosis**</font> n. 溃疡的形成

<font color="sky blue">**elcosis**</font> n. 溃疡的形成 

<font color="sky blue">**helciform**</font> adj. 溃疡状的

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid**</font> adj. 溃疡状的

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid carcinoma**</font> 溃疡状癌（一种类似溃疡的癌症）

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid tumor**</font> 溃疡状肿瘤

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid scar**</font> 溃疡状疤痕

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid wound**</font> 溃疡状伤口 

<font color="sky blue">**helcoid rash**</font> 溃疡状皮疹

<font color="sky blue">**helcodermatosis**</font> n. 溃疡性皮肤病

<font color="sky blue">**helcoma**</font> n. 角膜溃疡

<font color="sky blue">**dacryelcosis**</font> n. 泪器溃疡

<font color="sky blue">**dacryohelcosis**</font> n. 泪器溃疡
