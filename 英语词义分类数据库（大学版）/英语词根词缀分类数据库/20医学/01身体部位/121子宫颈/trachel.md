<font color="sky blue">**trachelitis**</font> n. 颈部炎症；子宫颈炎 

<font color="sky blue">**endotrachelitis**</font> n. 子宫颈内膜炎

<font color="sky blue">**hematotrachelos**</font> n. 子宫颈积血

<font color="sky blue">**trachelorrhaphy**</font> n. 子宫颈缝合术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelorrhaphy**</font> n. 宫颈裂伤缝合术

<font color="sky blue">**tracheloplasty**</font> n. 子宫颈成形术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotracheloplasty**</font> n. 子宫颈成形术

<font color="sky blue">**trachelectomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切除术（切除子宫颈和阴道的上部）

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelectomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切除术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelotomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切开术
