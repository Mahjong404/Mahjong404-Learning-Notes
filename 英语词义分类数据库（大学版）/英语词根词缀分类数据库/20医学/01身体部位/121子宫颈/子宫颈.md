**<center>1. trachel</center>**

- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">含义：子宫颈</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">变体：trachelo</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">来源：希腊单词trachēlos（意为neck，即脖子）</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">用途：构成子宫颈相关词汇</font>

<font color="sky blue">**trachelitis**</font> n. 颈部炎症；子宫颈炎 

<font color="sky blue">**endotrachelitis**</font> n. 子宫颈内膜炎

<font color="sky blue">**hematotrachelos**</font> n. 子宫颈积血

<font color="sky blue">**trachelorrhaphy**</font> n. 子宫颈缝合术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelorrhaphy**</font> n. 宫颈裂伤缝合术

<font color="sky blue">**tracheloplasty**</font> n. 子宫颈成形术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotracheloplasty**</font> n. 子宫颈成形术

<font color="sky blue">**trachelectomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切除术（切除子宫颈和阴道的上部）

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelectomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切除术

<font color="sky blue">**hysterotrachelotomy**</font> n. 子宫颈切开术

**<center>2. cervic</center>**

- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">含义：子宫颈</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">变体：cervico</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">来源：拉丁单词cervix（意为neck或nape，即脖子或颈背）</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">用途：构成子宫颈相关词汇</font>

<font color="sky blue">**cervical**</font> adj. 子宫颈的

<font color="sky blue">**cervicitis**</font> n. 宫颈炎，子宫颈炎

<font color="sky blue">**cervical cancer**</font> 子宫颈癌，宫颈癌

<font color="sky blue">**cervicoscope**</font> n. 子宫颈镜

<font color="sky blue">**cervicectomy**</font> n. 宫颈切除术，子宫颈切除术
