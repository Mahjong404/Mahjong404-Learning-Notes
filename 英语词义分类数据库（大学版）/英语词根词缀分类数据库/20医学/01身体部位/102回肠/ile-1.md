- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">含义：回肠</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">变体：ileo</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">来源：拉丁单词ileum（意为the lower part of the small intestine或the final and longest segment of the small intestine，即小肠下部或回肠）</font>
- ‌‌‌<font color="#f79646">用途：构成回肠相关的医学词汇</font>


---


<font color="sky blue">**ileum**</font> n. 回肠

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecal**</font> n. 回盲肠（指回肠和盲肠之间的交界处，其标志是回盲瓣）

<font color="sky blue">**ileocolic**</font> n. 回结肠（指回肠和结肠，通常用来描述它们在回盲瓣处连接的区域）

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecal sphincter**</font> 回盲肠括约肌（ileocecal valve的别称，它调节从小肠到大肠的物质流动）

<font color="sky blue">**ileitis**</font> n. 回肠炎

<font color="sky blue">**ileocolitis**</font> n. 回肠结肠炎

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecal tuberculosis**</font> 回盲肠结核

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecal intussusception**</font> 回肠盲肠套迭

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecectomy**</font> n. 回盲肠切除术

<font color="sky blue">**ileocecal resection**</font> 回盲肠切除术

<font color="sky blue">**ileostomy**</font> n. 回肠造口术
